About Me ​

Currently in my third year at Ashoka University, I am a Biology major with a minor in Mathematics. During my time here, I have had the opportunity to dive into experimental biology; specifically evolutionary immunology along with highly rigorous mathematics. Thanks to this exposure, I have developed an interest in dynamical living systems, and hope to couple experimental work with mathematical modelling and analysis. My current areas of interest lie in host-pathogen interactions, infection dynamics, disease ecology and transmission dynamics.

Outside of academics, I love eating and reading! I also really want to travel; in fact, one of my dreams is to eat my way across the world. I have been crocheting for the past year and dabbling with a little macrame as well. I have been gymming for more than three years now, and my goal for this year is to do at least one pull up!
